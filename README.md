## ✦ The Problem
You don't have a language problem. You have a **workflow problem**. 
Whenever you hit a sentence you don't understand, or need to rewrite an email, you have to break your flow: copy the text, switch to a new tab, open ChatGPT, paste it, wait, read, copy the answer, switch back, and paste. **It's exhausting.**

## ✨ Enter Tinky
Tinky lives quietly in your system tray. It doesn't want to be a destination. It wants to be an invisible layer of intelligence over every app you already use.

**How it works:**
1. Select any text (in a browser, Word, Notion, Slack—anywhere).
2. Hit your custom shortcut.
3. A beautiful, contextual menu appears right where you're looking.

## ⚡ Custom Triggers (Your Personal Genie)
Tinky ships with 9 built-in powers, but its true magic lies in **Custom Triggers**. 

If you do something repeatedly—like turning meeting notes into action items, rewriting text for LinkedIn, or formatting citations—you can teach Tinky how to do it once. Write a prompt, name the trigger, and it becomes a permanent part of your menu. 

*You teach Tinky your language, your workflow, and your voice. And it remembers.*

## 🧠 Choose Your Brain
Unlike other apps that lock you into one AI, Tinky lets you choose the engine that powers your magic:
- **OpenRouter** (Recommended): Access dozens of models (Claude, Mistral, Llama) with one key.
- **OpenAI**: Connect directly to GPT-4o.
- **Gemini**: Power your workflow with Google's latest models.
- **Ollama**: Run models **100% locally** on your machine. No internet. No cloud. Complete privacy.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy First
Your workflow is your business. 
Tinky does not read your screen in the background. It only reads the specific text you highlight when you press the shortcut. If you choose to use local models via Ollama, zero data ever leaves your computer.

## 🚀 Installation & Setup

1. Download the latest Beta release from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NHCMMDMTR7S?hl=en-us&gl=IN&ocid=pdpshare).
2. Run the installer and launch Tinky.
3. Open the Settings menu from your system tray.
4. Input your preferred API key (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Gemini) or configure your local Ollama port.
5. Set your global keyboard shortcut.
6. Highlight some text and make a wish!
